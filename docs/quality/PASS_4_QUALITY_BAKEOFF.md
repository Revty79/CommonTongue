# Pass 4 — Translation quality bake-off and brain selection

Pass 4 completes an isolated offline text bake-off against approved Pass 3 baseline `a6d5a1089e3f1727757feddc6bc31d34ca883d80`. The conditional primary Pass 5 research recommendation is **dedicated MADLAD400-3B-MT Q4_K using the pinned Candle T5 runtime**. It has the strongest reviewed quality mix, but unresolved regional/domain errors and unverified Android costs prevent a shipping claim. **No useful low-resource winner was found.** No production adapter or Pass 5 implementation was begun.

The corpus has **196 cases, 98 per direction**, with 32 prior-context cases, 28 hint cases including unrelated-sense controls, 14 primary regional cases, 10 embedded-instruction cases and 43 primary categories. It preserves all original 32 Pass 2 records and covers numbers, names/businesses, addresses, phone digits, dates/times, decimals/units, quantities, negation, idioms, profanity, incomplete speech, multi-clause content, automotive/construction/agriculture, hospitality/travel/emergency and language-only medical vocabulary. [Specification and category breakdown](BENCHMARK_SPEC.md).

Five meaningful families/generations were tested: exact OPUS int8 pair, M2M100-418M locally converted int8, Qwen3.5 0.8B/2B Q4_K_M plus the 0.8B Q5 control, official Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct Q4_K_M, and Google MADLAD400-3B-MT Q4_K. Dedicated MT, direct contextual translation, source-plus-MT-draft post-editing and maximum-one verification/repair were compared. Nine main configurations run the complete corpus; E runs the same 68-case risk panel used for comparative assessments. Six contextual configurations also produce 360 ablation outputs; the two-template pilot produces 64 outputs and the precision control 16. Core study evidence totals 2,272 outputs, excluding resource/parity replays.

Quality assessments are **model-assisted by Codex, unblinded and not native/fluent-human reviewed**. They are not model-generated golds or exact-phrase matching. All 680 main risk-panel outputs have explicit dimension scores and severity/rationales; another 80 pilot/precision outputs are assessed. A separate 66-observation post-sweep diagnostic audit checks 22 structured/polarity cases across the baseline, MADLAD and 2B hybrid without pooling denominators. Full main-sweep outputs outside the 68-case panel remain without absolute semantic ratings. Blinded reviewer export is prepared, not a performed human study. [Review format](HUMAN_REVIEW_FORMAT.md).

On the comparable panel, OPUS has **6 critical / 22 major failures**; MADLAD **1 / 18**; the 2B hybrid **3 / 19**; verification/retry **5 / 19**. MADLAD's combined critical/major rate is 27.9% versus OPUS 41.2%, with zero observed panel omission findings and one invented technical attribute. OPUS has three omission and one invention finding. Other configurations, dimension details, material/partial counts and exact denominators are in [candidate results](CANDIDATE_RESULTS.md). These difficult-panel rates are not general-use rates or qualified linguistic ground truth.

Context improves 4/32 cases for the 2B hybrid, leaves 27 unchanged and worsens one. Its hints improve 16/28, leave 10 unchanged and worsen two. Other direct/hybrid configurations also show adverse effects, including prior-turn copying, lost current clauses and unrelated-sense hint override. MADLAD supports neither context nor terminology in this tested interface. Regional weaknesses persist: feria/cash, cimbra/formwork in MADLAD, chamba/de la fregada and hacer el paro. MADLAD handles the reviewed Argentine colectivo case and preserves several ordinary full-clause examples, but no native conversational-naturalness or broad regional competence is established.

Direct small-model failures include changed written amounts, unsafe domain senses, copied source languages, omitted clauses, changed prohibitions and source obedience. The verifier produces 10 PASS replies on drafts assessed major/critical, 14 FAIL replies on none/minor drafts and 28 capped verdicts; its 27 repairs can introduce new polarity failures. Model confidence is not an independent meaning guarantee. Two controlled templates and the Q4/Q5 subset do not rescue the direct small-model approach under these pinned greedy/non-thinking settings.

The corrected four-thread MADLAD replay has **1.85-second warm median / 2.75-second p95**, **2.72 GiB sampled peak / 2.62 GiB steady process-tree RSS**, **1.13-second model-constructor load** and **1.67 GB active inference assets**. OPUS has 0.48 / 0.69 seconds, 0.97 GiB peak and 0.23 GB assets. The 2B hybrid has 2.63 / 3.53 seconds and 2.85 GiB peak; sequential reload reduces its measured peak to 1.95 GiB at a 4.84-second median on the smaller repeated-case study. Every 24-case resource replay exactly reproduces its matching quality output. [Architecture/resource comparison and go/no-go](ARCHITECTURE_COMPARISON.md).

The initial Candle quantized pool used six physical cores despite requesting Rayon=4. The worker now sets both pools to four; all 68 reviewed MADLAD outputs and the 24 replay outputs remain byte-identical. Initial metadata/control measurements are retained and labelled, and the final resource table uses corrected measurements. Other limitations include warm filesystem caches, excluded Python import/startup cost, sampled RSS rather than Android PSS, approximate CPU time and no ASR/TTS/app co-residency, thermals, battery or physical-phone data. Desktop memory/latency is not phone suitability.

All candidate translation/verifier inference is local. Public-host socket canaries fail with Windows 10013 in the restricted process sandbox; all post-tripwire attempts are zero. Native children inherit the OS restriction, workers have no download/server/cloud fallback interface, and tokenizers use local assets. Corrected-finalist and sequential-memory runs also pass these checks. Downloads were explicit preparation operations; no external inference/grading API or online competitor was invoked.

Every tested candidate has publisher, immutable revisions, filenames, SHA-256, tokenizer/runtime licenses, source/conversion provenance, commercial/redistribution obligations and ambiguity recorded. **57 local artifact sizes/digests were verified.** All seven configurations are provisional A by declared grants, never legally approved for production. M2M is MIT; the other models Apache-2.0. ONNX/CT2/llama.cpp are MIT; Candle MIT OR Apache-2.0. Third-party Qwen calibration/base-version provenance, official conversion tool/source commits and the MADLAD quantizer recipe remain release-review gaps. [Registry](../../tools/translation-quality/candidates.json), [lock](../../tools/translation-quality/artifacts.lock.json), [model policy](../MODEL_POLICY.md).

Local validation passed: **101 JVM tests, 6 existing research tests and 42 new model-free benchmark/evidence tests**, with no failures/skips; Gradle clean, test, lint, spotlessCheck, verifyCoreBoundaries, :app:verifyFoundationManifest, assembleDebug and :app:assembleDebugAndroidTest. Native workers were built and exercised offline; the llama.cpp official example reproduces a selected weak output byte for byte. Full Android instrumentation/physical-phone testing was not run in this research pass. Workflow syntax and final text/scope checks are recorded in [validation](PASS_4_VALIDATION.md); the pushed commit and actual remote Actions outcome are reported in the completion message.

Only isolated research, evidence/docs, model-policy/README links and a lightweight Python CI step are added. App/core source and Gradle dependencies, permissions, existing Pass 2 research/history and Pass 3 contracts remain unchanged. No hard contract mismatch required correction; future providers need honest semantic coverage, word-number/unit/polarity/clause verification and native cancellation/lifecycle work. Follow the [reproduction README](../../tools/translation-quality/README.md) for model-free checks, explicit preparation, offline runs and blinded export.

Pass 5 should probe the selected locked T5 format/runtime on Android ARM, recheck regressions and held-out fluent-reviewed regional/domain utterances, and measure latency, memory/coexistence, cancellation, lifecycle, OOM, thermals and battery. OPUS is a control; the 2B hybrid is an optional context/terminology comparator. There is no finalized tier mapping and no later-pass implementation in this commit.
