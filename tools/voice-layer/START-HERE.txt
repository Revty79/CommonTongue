COMMON TONGUE - PASS 6 PLAYBACK CORRECTION CHECK

1. Download CommonTongue-Pass6-Offline-Voice-Check.apk on the S25 and open it to update.
   Keep the existing app installed. This update is version 0.6.1-pass6-dev.
2. Open Common Tongue Voice Check. This is separate from the Pass 5 research app.
3. Set media volume to an audible level. Wi-Fi/cellular can remain on.
4. Tap Run English + Spanish checks. Listen for English, Spanish, then Spanish again (replay).
5. Wait for Checks finished, then tap Export Pass 6 results and save the JSON.
6. Share the JSON and say whether you heard English, Spanish and replay on the phone speaker.
   The individual Hear English, Hear Spanish and Replay buttons remain available if needed.

Keep your Pass 5 app, data, test pack, and offline voices. No reimport or model download.
No computer, ADB, microphone permission, account, or paid service is required.
If an offline voice is missing, tap Open phone voice settings and install the language there.
Vendor voices are installed by the phone/vendor, never copied into Common Tongue.
This is a debug test build for authorized devices, not a consumer production release.
Passing automated checks does not replace your confirmation that you heard the speech.
