COMMON TONGUE - PASS 6 OFFLINE VOICE CHECK

1. Download CommonTongue-Pass6-Offline-Voice-Check.apk on the S25 and open it to install.
2. Open Common Tongue Voice Check. This is separate from the Pass 5 research app.
3. Set media volume to an audible level. Wi-Fi/cellular can remain on.
4. Tap Run English + Spanish checks and listen for English and Spanish.
5. Wait for Checks finished. The app also checks replay, rapid replacement, and safe failures.
6. Try Hear English, Hear Spanish, Replay last speech, and Stop speaking.
7. If available, repeat through headphones/Bluetooth, then disconnect during speech.
   Speech should stop instead of spilling onto the phone speaker.
8. Leave the app during speech, return, and try Replay last speech or a new turn.
9. Optionally repeat Run English + Spanish checks with Airplane Mode ON as offline proof.
10. Tap Export Pass 6 results, save the JSON, and share it with your coordinator.
    Also say whether you heard English and Spanish, and which output devices you used.

Keep your Pass 5 app, data, test pack, and offline voices. No reimport or model download.
No computer, ADB, microphone permission, account, or paid service is required.
If an offline voice is missing, tap Open phone voice settings and install the language there.
Vendor voices are installed by the phone/vendor, never copied into Common Tongue.
This is a debug test build for authorized devices, not a consumer production release.
Passing automated checks does not replace your confirmation that you heard the speech.
